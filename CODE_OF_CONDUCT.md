# Code of Conduct

## In short

Be decent. This is a small tool for a small trade; treat contributors and users
the way you would treat someone standing next to you at a machine.

## Expected

- Assume good faith. Most "wrong" files are a misunderstanding, not carelessness.
- Critique the output, the geometry, the measurement — not the person.
- Say what you measured. "Fidelity dropped to 78 % on `text_serif`" beats "this
  broke it".
- Remember that people arrive here mid-job with a part on the bed and a
  customer waiting. Impatience is not hostility.

## Not acceptable

Harassment, personal attacks, discriminatory language, deliberate derailing,
or publishing others' private information.

## Enforcement

Report problems to the maintainers via a
[private security advisory](https://github.com/JuicedSystems/lasertrace-pro/security/advisories/new)
or by email through the GitHub profile. Maintainers may edit, remove or reject
contributions and comments that violate this document, and may ban repeat
offenders.
